We are building a premium web platform for a medical school admission preparation course (MOR exam). The platform must simulate the real biographical exam experience as closely as possible.\

- Professional\
- Technological\
- Elite and selective\
- Slightly stressful (in a controlled, motivating way)\

- 13 structured questions\

- Arabic\
- Hebrew\
- English\

- Medical theme (white, blue, clean UI)\

- Minimalist but powerful\
- Serious tone (NOT friendly or childish)\

- Use uploaded logo: "Apsos MOR"\

TASK:\
Build a complete modern web platform with the following features:\

1. LANDING PAGE:\

- Call-to-action: "Start Simulation"\

  - Success statistics\
